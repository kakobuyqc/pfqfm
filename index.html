What is a Litbuy spreadsheet?

A Litbuy spreadsheet is a product research sheet used to organize shopping links and notes. Useful fields include category, source URL, selected variant, measurements, listed price, currency and the date each detail was checked. It is a research aid, not proof of stock, authenticity or platform support.

Litbuy shopping guide

Build a shortlist you can actually use.

The most useful Litbuy spreadsheet finds are the ones you can compare consistently. A striking image may earn a place on your shortlist; clear measurements and source information help you decide whether it should stay there.

Keep your research focused on a small group of comparable items. A lightweight hoodie and a heavy knit sweater can share a category while serving different purposes.

Start browsing ↗

  1. Choose the role of the item

    Decide whether you need everyday sneakers, a layering piece, training clothing or a finishing accessory. Separate must-have features from preferences.

  2. Save a specific product link

    For your Litbuy product links, record the original URL and exact variant. Keep color, size and material notes beside the link so that similar listings do not become interchangeable.

  3. Check fit with measurements

    Compare clothing measurements with a garment you already wear. For footwear, keep the size system and units visible. For caps and jewelry, record adjustment ranges or lengths.

  4. Separate item price from total cost

    Record currency and the date checked. Keep delivery estimates, service fees, potential taxes and conversion costs in separate fields rather than treating the item price as a final total.

  5. Recheck the source before purchasing

    Confirm current availability, variant details and return conditions. If you plan to use a shopping service, verify link support and item restrictions through that service before paying.

What is a Litbuy spreadsheet?

A Litbuy spreadsheet is a product research sheet used to organize shopping links and notes. Useful fields include category, source URL, selected variant, measurements, listed price, currency and the date each detail was checked. It is a research aid, not proof of stock, authenticity or platform support.

Can I download a spreadsheet from this page?

This page provides a browsing directory and a guide rather than a downloadable spreadsheet. Use the suggested columns to create your own research sheet, or browse the linked CAFANS categories.

Can every listed product link be used with Litbuy?

Do not assume that every source link is supported. Check the current platform interface, item restrictions and destination rules before paying. A directory link alone does not confirm compatibility.

How should I compare clothing and sneaker sizes?

For clothing, compare garment measurements with a similar item you own. For sneakers, check the listing's size chart, units and model-specific fit notes. A size label alone is not enough to establish fit.

Does a spreadsheet price include shipping and other costs?

Treat a listed item price as one component of the total. Check domestic delivery, service charges, international shipping, applicable taxes and currency conversion separately. Record estimates separately from confirmed charges.

Is this an official Litbuy or designer brand website?

No. This is an independent research guide directing visitors to CAFANS. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Litbuy, Nike, Louis Vuitton, Gucci or Hermès. Brand images are visual references, not claims about CAFANS stock or authenticity.
